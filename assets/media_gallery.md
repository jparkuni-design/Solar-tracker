# Media Gallery

Photos and video footage of the build, in action, and under test.

**[View the full gallery on Google Drive →](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Fn9SYc0reMR3OrqTbvjNLnqpVhTl2fSM?usp=sharing)**

The folder contains ~13 photos/videos covering:

- **Components** — close-up shots of the individual parts (LCD, photoresistors, DHT-11, servos, solar panel, wiring) as assembled on the tracker.
- **Functionality** — the dual-axis tracking in action, following a light source across both axes, with the LCD showing live sensor/power data.
- **Testing** — footage from the lab testing sessions used to gather the efficiency data referenced in the [Lab Results](../README.md#lab-results) section.
